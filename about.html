About Cusco

The ancient capital of the Inca empire, where living history meets vibrant modern life.

Heritage & Culture

Cusco blends Inca stonework, Spanish colonial architecture, and bustling modern energy. From ritual festivals to artisan workshops, the city celebrates its roots every day.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Gateway to Machu Picchu
  • Epicenter of Andean traditions

Why Visit Cusco?

Balance adventure, history, and gastronomy in one unforgettable destination.

Ancient Ruins

Explore fortified temples, sun shrines, and astronomical sites.

Vibrant Markets

Shop handwoven alpaca textiles, ceramics, and organic cacao.

Andean Cuisine

Savor local flavors like chiriuchu, quinoa soups, and Pisco sours.
